It preserves Evaluation 002 and imports its ten hash-verified scored artifacts
as a completed scientific prefix.  The predecessor’s provider settlements are
carried once as historical exposure; they are not recharged to successor arm
costs.  The first CoProMem Dynamic task-boundary transition is reconstructed
offline from exactly its two durable Dynamic artifacts, then checkpointed under
the successor manifest.

The scientific allocation, arms, trial order, original ReMe bank, and original
CoProMem bank are unchanged.  Later trajectories begin only after the imported
ReMe Dynamic prefix and reconstructed CoProMem boundary pass their maintained
fail-closed reconciliation checks.
