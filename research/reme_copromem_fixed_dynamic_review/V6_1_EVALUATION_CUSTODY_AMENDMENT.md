# V6.1 evaluation custody amendment

This pre-allocation, infrastructure/custody amendment replaces textual-ID
exclusion with a fail-closed behavior-based rule. A development task is hard
excluded only when durable evidence shows payload/state access, action
execution, scoring, history or native journal creation, executed-task
retrieval, executor settlement, or a task update/commit. Public inventory,
public descriptor, candidate-list, documentation, and withheld-task mentions
are not exposure by themselves. Structured evidence whose task custody cannot
be established remains excluded. `test_normal` is always prohibited.

This changes neither the immutable 24-trajectory acquisition pool, the ReMe
construction successor, the v6.1 CoProMem bank, model routes, five-arm design,
or analysis. Any resulting evaluation is compatibility-conditioned exploratory
sampling, never an unbiased benchmark sample or efficacy claim.
