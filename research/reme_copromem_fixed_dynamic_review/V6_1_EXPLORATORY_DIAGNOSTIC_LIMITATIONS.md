# V6.1 Exploratory Diagnostic Limitations

Any pilot using `copromem-v6.1-semantic-recovery-003` is exploratory only.
The frozen recovery is not confirmatory-quality memory because some schemas
retain acquisition-specific repeated-call cardinalities; guidance contains
operation constraints rather than full loop, filtering, branching,
aggregation, or rendered-dataflow semantics; retrieval selects the first
compatible schema rather than the highest-scoring compatible schema; some
schemas are redundant or yield identical guidance; and every committed schema
uses successful evidence only, without committed negative contrastive evidence.

The frozen bank must not be repaired, deleted, merged, reranked,
reinterpreted, or regenerated after a diagnostic manifest is frozen. Any
outcome may diagnose helpful, neutral, or harmful operation-only guidance, but
cannot establish confirmatory efficacy, superiority, generalization, or a
paper-level reproduction claim.
