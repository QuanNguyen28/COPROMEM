## Question, competing explanations and decision

Can one bounded, reflection-assisted fresh attempt supply additional usable
source contrasts where the unchanged no-memory collection has produced only
all-success or all-failure examples? The alternative is that binary feedback
and a short lesson do not overcome the team's task-competence bottleneck.
Hard tasks may remain unsolved; successful tasks may remain consistently solved.

The **primary metric** is the number of previously unmixed build scenarios that
gain a replay-eligible opposite-outcome retry. There are eight registered tasks,
seven previously unmixed scenarios, and one already mixed scenario (`aa8502b`).
That old mixed scenario cannot contribute new support. The metric uses every
eligible prior outcome in the two pinned corpora, not only the critic's one
input trajectory. Unsupported/provider-failed retries stay in the denominator
but cannot form induction pairs. Zero newly mixed scenarios falsifies sufficiency
of this protocol. Report the eight-task and seven-opportunity denominators.

**KEEP** this bounded source-acquisition component only if at least one new
mixed scenario emerges, the full registered sample is collected and integrity
audits pass. Otherwise **REVISE**; do not increase retries within this cycle,
switch tasks/models, reinterpret partial scores or count the old mixed pair.
Even KEEP means source evidence only, never contract admission or superiority.

This is not a randomized comparison of reflection against a fresh no-memory
draw. Any changed outcome could reflect the extra attempt/decoding variation,
textual guidance, or different effective history. Source sufficiency, not a
causal reflection-effect estimate, is the question. A subsequent learned-method
comparison must still include textual reflection and equal-compute retries.
