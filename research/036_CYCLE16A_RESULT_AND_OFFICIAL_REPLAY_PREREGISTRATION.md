## New question and primary decision

Can the eight unedited archived train-program sequences, executed in our local
canonical initial worlds, supply additional replay-eligible opposite outcomes
to the existing build corpus? Source competence, old-version behavior, hardcoded
observed values, language restrictions and nonportable namespace state are strong
competing explanations for failure. We do not know the archived native outcomes
and will not read them. New scores describe **local execution of saved programs**,
not reproduction of the original agent's performance or proof of source learning.

Primary metric: number of previously unmixed build tasks that become mixed after
adding one eligible local archived-source outcome to all eligible cycle-11/14/15
outcomes. Eight tasks, seven opportunities; old mixed aa8502b cannot count as
independent new support. Count failures, exclusions and unsupported sequences in
the registered denominator. Success-to-failure can supply contrast but is not a
quality improvement; report it explicitly. Duplicate replay executions provide
integrity evidence, not extra statistical samples or source-task support.

**KEEP** this offline source route for further local effect investigation only
if at least one new mixed task emerges, all eight records are attempted and
identity/scoring/accounting audits pass. Otherwise **REVISE**. No task replacement,
manual code fixes, answer substitution, different seed or additional attempt
may be selected after outcomes. A partial/infrastructure-failed sample cannot
satisfy the full-sample gate. Neither decision admits a contract or proves
contrast/scope/retrieval novelty, held-out advantage or publication readiness.
