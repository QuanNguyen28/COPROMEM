# CoProMem / CoProCon: claim–evidence ledger, 16 September 2026

This ledger accompanies the [status report](STATUS_REPORT_2026-09-16.md). It is a retrospective claim audit, not a new research protocol. The current AppWorld pilot remains killed; learned activation on the eleven fixed pairs remains closed with the user's final REVISE decision. No entry under “missing decisive evidence” authorizes obtaining it, collecting data, fitting another selector or executing a cycle.

## Interpretation

Status labels are deliberately asymmetric:

- **SUPPORTED:** directly supported within the stated finite implementation/sample boundary.
- **PRELIMINARY:** observed local signal, insufficient for the broader mechanism or population claim.
- **FALSIFIED:** contradicted for the exact stated formulation/sample; not automatically a universal negative.
- **INCONCLUSIVE:** appropriate comparison or sufficient independent evidence is missing.
- **NOT TESTED:** a proposed mechanism/comparison was not executed.
- **KILLED CONFIGURATION:** the specified experiment cannot proceed under its evidential/authorization constraints.

Independent-unit counts below are scenarios/tasks or authored template clusters. A repeated seed, action boundary, repair variant, request, branch or replay is not an independent task. “Paper-safe” refers to the exact limited wording in that row, not a suggested title-level novelty claim or publication guarantee. A measured absence of effect, an invalid experiment, and an unexecuted experiment are different things.
