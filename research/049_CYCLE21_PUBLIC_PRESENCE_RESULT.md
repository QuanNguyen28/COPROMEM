## Hostile-review decision and next experiment

Retain this limited common observation interface. Do not treat an ordinary
presence query as novel memory, or the native success-preservation gate as an
efficacy result. The next step separates **local missing-input violations** from
final task outcomes and measures what a standard static name checker can already
do with this public context. That is a necessary control component, not a
substitute for a strong manual semantic verifier.

Any later learned contract must add attributable value beyond such controls,
handle semantic errors rather than only missing names, learn or validate scope,
survive successful-origin recovery checks and transfer to untouched groups.
The two earlier local repairs remain unadmitted. No learned stateful contract
bank or held-out method advantage exists; the full research goal remains active.
